DATABASE SCHEMA
================================================================================

NEW FIRESTORE COLLECTIONS:

1. paymentRequests
   - Tracks pending and completed payment requests
   - 30-minute expiry
   - Fields: userId, amount, currency, description, source, sourceId, status

2. paymentHistory
   - Full payment records for history/reporting
   - Fields: userId, requestId, amount, currency, description, source, 
            method, status, cardUsed, receiptId, verificationCode, paidAt

3. savedCards
   - User's saved payment methods
   - Fields: userId, cardNumber, cardholderName, lastFourDigits, 
            expiryMonth/Year, isDefault, createdAt
